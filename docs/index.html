THE IDEA BEHIND VIT CONNECT

03 WHY IT EXISTS

Built out of a
real problem.

Campus information
shouldn't die in a chat.

Every feature in VIT Connect exists to fix one recurring failure that every campus already has.

01 / NOT FINDABLE

It can't be searched

A notice posted in April is invisible in September. Scrollback is not a search engine, and anyone who joined after the announcement never saw it at all.

02 / FRAGMENTED

It gets retyped five times

The same announcement lands in the course group, the year group and three department chats. Some people get it. Most don't. Nobody can tell which copy is current.

03 / NO AUTHORITY

Nothing says who said it

A screenshot of a schedule change carries no author and no date. So nobody can tell whether it is still true, and the wrong version gets acted on.

Put in one public, searchable, attributed thread, all three problems cost nothing to fix.
