Privacy Policy

VIT Connect is a student-built campus forum. This page explains exactly what the platform stores, why it stores it, and how to get it removed.

Last updated: 11 October 2026 · Applies to vit-connect.vercel.app and real-ds.github.io/vit-connect

1. What we collect

VIT Connect collects only what the product needs to function. There is no analytics, no advertising SDK, and no third-party tracking script on this site.

  • Account data. Your email address and display name, supplied by Firebase Authentication when you sign in with email/password or Google.
  • Profile data. Anything you choose to add yourself: department, bio, a resume URL, and LinkedIn or GitHub links. Every one of these fields is optional and editable or removable at any time.
  • Content you create. Posts, the communities you join, and the communities you create.
  • A basic audit trail. Each record carries a creation timestamp so posts and profiles can be ordered and dated.

2. Why we use it

  • To authenticate you and keep you signed in.
  • To show the feed, communities and posts you have access to.
  • To attribute content to an author and date it.
  • To let you correct or delete anything you have posted.

Your email address is never displayed publicly on VIT Connect and is never sold or shared for marketing.

3. Who processes your data

Authentication and storage are handled by Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication). Your data is stored on Google infrastructure and is subject to the Firebase Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

If you sign in with Google, your Google account name, email and profile photo are shared with VIT Connect at your request, and with Firebase in order to complete sign-in. This landing page is hosted on GitHub Pages, which serves static files only and receives standard server request logs.

4. Cookies and local storage

This landing page sets no cookies. The VIT Connect application stores your Firebase authentication session in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in between visits. Clearing your browser data signs you out.

5. Your rights and choices

You can view and edit your own profile at any time. To exercise any of the following, open an issue on the repository or contact the maintainer directly:

  • Access a copy of the data associated with your account.
  • Correct information that is inaccurate.
  • Request permanent deletion of your account and its associated posts.
  • Withdraw a profile field you previously published.

6. Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account exists. If you request deletion, your profile and the content you authored are removed from Firestore. Removal from Firebase backup snapshots can take up to a further 30 days to propagate.

7. Children

VIT Connect is intended for college students, faculty and staff. It is not directed at anyone under 18, and accounts belonging to under-18 users should be deleted on request.

8. Security

Access is restricted to signed-in members and traffic is served over HTTPS. Firestore security rules are part of the planned roadmap; until they are enforced, treat the platform as a closed community space rather than a confidential one, and do not post anything you would not want every classmate to see.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above changes with it, and the revision history stays visible in the public repository.

A note on this document. VIT Connect is an open-source student project, not a commercial service, and this policy is written by the project maintainer rather than by a lawyer. It is accurate as a description of what the code does today. If the project is ever run at scale or made commercial, this page must be reviewed by qualified counsel.

10. Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/real-ds/vit-connect/issues, or reach the maintainer at github.com/real-ds.
